# Asset Register Assessment Report

## Executive Summary

An Information Asset Register was developed to provide visibility into information and associated assets supporting business operations.

The register contains 10 assets across information, applications, infrastructure, endpoints and services.

A Python-based validation process was implemented to improve data quality and automatically calculate asset criticality.

---

## Key Findings

### Critical Assets

Assets with the highest CIA requirements require enhanced protection and recovery capabilities.

### Personal Data

Several assets contain or process personal data.

These assets should be considered during GDPR Data Mapping activities.

### Internet-Facing Assets

Internet-facing assets require additional security controls and monitoring.

### Risk Linkage

Assets are linked to corresponding risk identifiers where applicable.

---

## Recommendations

1. Review asset ownership periodically.
2. Maintain accurate asset lifecycle status.
3. Review CIA classifications following significant changes.
4. Ensure critical assets have appropriate backup controls.
5. Link critical assets to the risk register.
6. Include personal-data assets in GDPR data mapping.
7. Periodically review internet-facing assets.

---

## Conclusion

The Asset Register provides a structured foundation for information security governance and supports future risk management, privacy, incident response and business continuity activities.
