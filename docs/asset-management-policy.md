# Asset Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for maintaining an accurate inventory of information and associated assets.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to information, applications, infrastructure, endpoints, services and other assets supporting business processes.

## 3. Asset Registration

Relevant assets shall be recorded in the organization's Asset Register.

Each asset shall have a unique Asset ID.

## 4. Asset Ownership

Each relevant asset shall have an assigned Asset Owner.

A Custodian should also be assigned where operational or technical management is required.

## 5. Classification

Assets shall be assessed according to confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.

Information shall also receive an appropriate information classification.

## 6. Lifecycle

Assets should be tracked throughout their lifecycle:

- Active
- Under Review
- Inactive
- Retired

## 7. Review

The Asset Register should be reviewed periodically and following significant changes.

## 8. Risk Management

Assets shall be considered during information security risk assessments.

Where appropriate, assets should be linked to risk records.

## 9. Responsibilities

### IT Manager

Responsible for technical asset inventory management.

### Asset Owners

Responsible for business importance and security requirements.

### GRC / Security Function

Responsible for governance, monitoring and periodic review.

### Employees

Responsible for reporting unidentified or new assets.

## 10. Evidence

Evidence may include:

- Asset Register
- Ownership records
- Review records
- Change records
- Risk references
- Asset retirement records
